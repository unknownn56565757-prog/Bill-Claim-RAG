MEAL REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Purpose
This policy defines the rules for reimbursement of meal expenses incurred during approved business travel.

Eligibility
Employees on officially approved business travel are eligible to claim meal expenses.

Reimbursable Expenses
The following meal expenses are reimbursable:

• Breakfast, lunch, and dinner during approved travel.
• Business meals with clients, partners, or customers.
• Reasonable snacks during long-distance travel exceeding 6 hours.
• Taxes and mandatory service charges included in meal bills.

Daily Limits
The maximum reimbursable meal amount is:

• Breakfast: $15
• Lunch: $25
• Dinner: $40

The total daily claim amount must not exceed $80 per day unless prior management approval has been obtained.

Receipt Requirements
Employees must provide:

• Original itemized receipts.
• Date of purchase.
• Restaurant or vendor information.
• Amount paid.

Claims submitted without valid receipts may be rejected.

Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following expenses are not reimbursable:

• Alcoholic beverages.
• Personal entertainment expenses.
• Meals for family members or friends.
• Room-service luxury charges unrelated to business travel.
• Excessive or unreasonable meal expenses.

Claim Submission
Meal claims must be submitted within 30 days of trip completion.

Approval Process
All meal reimbursement claims are subject to manager approval and finance verification.

Policy Violations
Submission of false, altered, or duplicate receipts may result in claim rejection and disciplinary action.

Examples

Example 1:
Employee spends $18 for breakfast and submits a valid receipt.
Result: Reimbursable.

Example 2:
Employee purchases alcoholic beverages worth $25 during dinner.
Result: Not reimbursable.

Example 3:
Employee spends $95 on meals in a single day without prior approval.
Result: Amount exceeding the daily limit may be rejected.
